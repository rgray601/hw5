Three problems this team cannot take yet

Actually sell the hoodies. Ticket 103 declined a discount and the Cash tab only has money going out: $840.00, then $2,400.00. Eight hoodies at the $58.00 list price would be $464.00 coming in. Receive the reprint and tell Tauhid it shipped. Ticket 101 ended with size S still at 0 and a purchase-order draft sitting in the notes. Name a real bulk price and hold the eight hoodies. Ticket 103 said “bulk discount” and never named a unit price.
